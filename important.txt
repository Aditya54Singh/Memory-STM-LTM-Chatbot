Why convert SearchItem to RetrievedMemory?

This is an important design decision.

SearchItem is an internal LangGraph/PostgresStore object. If you use it throughout your application, your business logic becomes tightly coupled to the storage implementation.

By converting it to your own RetrievedMemory model:

Your nodes and services depend only on your domain models.
If you later switch from PostgresStore to another vector database (e.g., Qdrant, Pinecone, Weaviate), only the retrieval service needs to change.
The rest of your application remains unchanged.

This follows the Dependency Inversion Principle from SOLID.
